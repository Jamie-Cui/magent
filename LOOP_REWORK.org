* Objective and constraints

Implement the approved plan: ordered messages and provider replay, explicit
turn completion, useful progress communication, and a ledger-backed
update_plan tool. Keep gptel-request as the transport and agent-shell/ACP as
the frontend. Breaking internal contracts is authorized; persisted user data
must be preserved. Do not add a judge, hidden summary requests, or another
execution engine.
